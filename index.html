Robert Wu develops landscape strategies that connect ecology, climate adaptation and social life across urban and territorial scales.

Grounds in the Making — Campus Acupuncture

Building upon the existing landscape, the project introduces a series of carefully positioned interventions that reconnect movement, encourage interaction and reveal overlooked spaces. Rather than transforming the campus through a single gesture, a lightweight and adaptable system gradually weaves its fragmented parts into a shared and evolving landscape.
