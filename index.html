Landscape as Living Structure

My work explores landscape as an active system rather than a static object. Through ecological urbanism, landscape infrastructure and research-by-design, I investigate how ecological processes, public life and spatial systems can be integrated into adaptive landscapes. Rather than creating isolated forms, I aim to design frameworks that evolve with environmental and social change.
