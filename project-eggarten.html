Project 01 — Ecological Urbanism

Eggarten Forest City

A forest-oriented residential district that treats existing woodland as primary spatial infrastructure rather than residual greenery. The proposal integrates ecological continuity, collective life and long-term urban transformation.

Location

Munich, Germany

Year

2025–26

Type

Academic · TUM

Team

Robert Wu · Anton Lochner · Moby Siivonen

Forest as infrastructure

Eggarten is understood as a threshold between ecological corridors, native habitats, urban infrastructure and emerging forms of habitation. The project reverses the conventional hierarchy between building and landscape by embedding residential clusters within a continuous woodland matrix.

Existing trees and new planting layers structure circulation, microclimate and spatial hierarchy. Green corridors connect public forest, communal spaces, gardens and semi-private pockets while supporting ecological exchange across the site.
